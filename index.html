Research Interests

Algorithm Configuration

Automatically tuning and selecting algorithms instead of hand-tweaking them. I am interested in configuration procedures with provable guarantees, algorithm selection, and empirical performance models for solvers of hard combinatorial problems.

Algorithmic Game Theory

Computation in settings where participants act strategically. I am interested in mechanism and market design, equilibrium computation, and using learning methods to design rules that lead to good collective outcomes.

Knowledge Representation and Reasoning

Logic-based representations that enable automated reasoning. I am interested in declarative problem solving such as Answer Set Programming, and in combining symbolic reasoning with learning for more explainable AI.

Multi-Agent Systems

How autonomous agents coordinate, communicate, and plan together. I am interested in decentralized planning under uncertainty, epistemic reasoning about what agents know, and fair, effective coordination mechanisms.
